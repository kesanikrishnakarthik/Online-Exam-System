🏛️ Multi-Tenant Architecture & Cloud Isolation Protocol

In this academic DevOps project, every educational institution acts as a logically isolated tenant partitioned by its unique collegeCode. All examinations, question banks, student attempts, and academic scores are strictly isolated server-side through Spring Data JPA repository specifications and client-side Firestore security rules. A faculty member or student belonging to Institution A can never query, observe, or manipulate examinations hosted by Institution B.
